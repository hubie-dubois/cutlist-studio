A little precision goes a long way.

Saw kerf
The material your blade removes with every cut. Check your blade’s actual cutting width.
Edge trim
A margin removed from all four sides before the layout begins. Trim cuts are separate from the numbered cut sequence.
Grain & rotation
Length runs horizontally on the drawing. Choose the length or width grain direction for each part and stock sheet. Parts may rotate only if the grain directions still align. Material names match without case sensitivity.
Optimization
We compare up to 512 straight-cut packing strategies (128 above 1,000 pieces; 80 above 2,500). The progress panel tracks the actual search and the best layout found so far. Choose Use best so far to accept the current layout, or Cancel optimization to stop. This is a practical heuristic, not a guarantee of the mathematical minimum. Offcuts, kerf, and trim are included in unused area.
Inches & fractions
Enter decimals or fractions such as 3/4 and 24 1/2. Feet and inches accept 2' 3 1/2"; unmarked numbers in feet/inch modes are inches. Fractions display to the nearest 1/64 inch. Changing display units preserves the underlying dimensions.

Saved lists

Loading replaces the current list. Undo is available after loading.

Paste spreadsheet rows

Paste tab-separated cells or CSV. Use a header row, or columns: length, width, quantity, label, material. Dimensions use the current units unless a unit column is included.
